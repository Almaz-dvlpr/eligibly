How it works

  1. Pick your countryWe compute your verdict from each job’s hiring rules: YES, UNCLEAR or NO.
  2. See the proofEvery YES shows the exact sentence from the posting. No quote, no YES.
  3. Get your packYour details and standard answers, ready to paste into the company’s form.
  4. You take it from thereWe never write to employers or track your replies.

Where we stop. Eligibly helps you find jobs you can actually take and get your application ready. It does not email employers, answer recruiters or track your replies. After you click Apply, the process is yours.
Eligibly is an early prototype. Verdicts come from a rule-based reader of public postings and can be wrong: check the quoted line and the posting before you apply. Data: public Greenhouse job boards, refreshed on demand.

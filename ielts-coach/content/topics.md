# IELTS Writing Task 2 — 52 Topics and 260 Essay Questions

**Practice collection:** 52 common IELTS themes × 5 essay prompts each = **260 questions**.

These are original practice questions modelled on common IELTS Writing Task 2 formats, **not an official or verified list of past exam questions**. Aim for at least 250 words per essay and approximately 40 minutes of writing time.

## 1. Education — Образование
1. Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
2. Some people think that schools should focus more on practical skills than academic subjects. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
3. Online learning is becoming more popular than traditional classroom education. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
4. Many students experience high levels of stress because of examinations. What are the causes, and what solutions can you suggest?
5. Some people believe that children should start learning a foreign language at primary school. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

## 2. Work and Career — Работа и карьера
